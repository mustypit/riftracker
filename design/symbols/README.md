Rules-text symbols for the card library, 24x24, `currentColor` (domain colour in text).
Domain emblems are traced from OpenRift's `/images/domains/<domain>.webp`; might and the any-domain
rune (`rainbow`, gradient over OpenRift's `power.svg` shape) are OpenRift's SVGs; exhaust is redrawn
on a trace of the printed card. Energy is drawn in code: a white circle with the number in dark.
`../openrift-symbols/` holds the downloaded references (not shipped).
